The support was very hard to remove.

[2026-08-10 Mon] I needed to replace the one of my back pack that broke

https://makerworld.com/en/models/819280-strong-printable-snap-buckle#profileId-762092

I needed a 40mm buckle.

I took the 3.2cm, rotated the female by 90°, then scaled up to 110% on the X axis.

Then, to make the female part fit, I needed a strap buckle.

I tried https://makerworld.com/en/models/1980259-belt-strap-clip-38mm-thin-and-thick

But it did not work very well

Then I tried https://makerworld.com/en/models/669780-strap-adjuster-buckle-25mm

With 160% scale in X
