Weekly Review Is a Necessary Cost of Being in Cruise Control

Fleeting

weekly review is a necessary cost of being in cruise control

When I tell people I take up to 2 hours a week to perform my weekly review, I generally get the reaction that it is a waste of time.

I think it is a necessary cost to get stuff out of my head. It looks obvious (in retrospect) that in order for stuff to be outside of my head, I need to take the time to get those out.
