A New Battery for the ATJ 8466 Portable Car Tire Air Pump (Compresseur)

Fleeting

Context

  • Device: ATJ 8466 portable car tire air pump1
  • Current battery: 2x 2000mAh lithium (original, may need replacement)
  • Goal: Find 2 suitable 18650 batteries within €15 budget
  • Key requirement: Minimum 3.5C discharge (7A ÷ 2Ah)2

Battery Specs Needed

  • Capacity: 2000-2600 mAh per cell (used in pairs for 7.4V total)
  • Discharge rate: Minimum 3.5C (7A ÷ 2Ah), ideally 5-10C for safety margin2
  • Voltage: 3.7V nominal per cell (7.4V when paired in series)
  • Form: 18650 flat-top
  • Quantity: 2 cells (required for ATJ 8466)1
  • Power: Device draws 50W maximum3

Options Compared

XINLANTECH 5200mAh 1s2p (€16.99) — Amazon.fr4

  • Specifications: 5200mAh total (1S2P = 2 cells parallel)5
  • Discharge: 5C (~10A max)6 — too low for compressor
  • Verdict: ❌ Insufficient

JESSPOW 3000mAh 30A (€28.79/4 cells) — Amazon.fr7

  • Specifications: 3000mAh, 30A continuous discharge8
  • Cost for 2: €14.40 (~€7.20 each) — within €15 budget
  • Actual draw: 7A ÷ 3Ah = 2.3C — only 23% of rated 30A capacity
  • Running at 23% rated discharge is gentle on the cell, promotes longevity
  • Only product found on Amazon.fr with explicitly stated discharge rate (searches: «pile 18650 rechargeable flat top», «18650 haute décharge plat», «18650 flat top 20A» — all others were chargers, packs with connectors, or NiMH cells)9
  • Verdict: ✅ RECOMMENDED — within budget, verified specs, favorable for longevity
  • Capacity sufficient for 15-30 min compressor runtime per charge10

Samsung INR18650-25R 2500mAh 20A — nkon.nl only11

  • Specifications: 2500mAh, 20A discharge, 3.6V, flat top12
  • Cost for 2: €3.90 (€1.95/cell at nkon.nl)
  • Discharge: 20A ÷ 2.5Ah = 8C — 2.3× margin over 3.5C minimum
  • ⚠️ Not found on Amazon.fr
  • Verdict: ✅ Suitable — reputable brand, verified specs, well within budget

DMEGC INR18650-25P 2500mAh 20A — nkon.nl only13

  • Specifications: 2500mAh, 20A discharge, 3.6V, flat top14
  • Cost for 2: €3.30 (€1.65/cell at nkon.nl) — cheapest option with verified specs
  • Discharge: 20A ÷ 2.5Ah = 8C — 2.3× margin over 3.5C minimum
  • ⚠️ Not found on Amazon.fr
  • Verdict: ✅ Suitable — less known brand but specs verified on product page

LG INR18650-HJ2 3000mAh 20A — nkon.nl only15

  • Specifications: 3000mAh, 20A discharge, 3.6V, flat top16
  • Cost for 2: €6.98 (€3.49/cell at nkon.nl)
  • Discharge: 20A ÷ 3Ah = 6.67C — well above 3.5C minimum
  • 20% more capacity than 2500mAh cells → longer runtime per charge
  • 3000mAh is the practical ceiling for 18650 cells meeting the discharge requirement17
  • ⚠️ Not found on Amazon.fr
  • Verdict: ✅ BEST LONGEVITY — most capacity available while meeting discharge spec

JESSPOW 3300mAh (€25.80/4) — Amazon.fr18

  • Discharge: “high” but unspecified ⚠️
  • Cost for 2: €13
  • Verdict: ⚠️ Risky — no confirmed discharge rate for high-drain device19

9V alkaline (PP3 / 6LR61) — rejected

  • Nominal voltage: 9V — ~22% above the device’s 7.4V nominal, risk of damaging motor driver / electronics20
  • Capacity: ~550 mAh per cell21 — 4× below the 2000 mAh minimum
  • Practical continuous current: ~30 mA for any sensible lifespan22 — the compressor draws 7000 mA (~230× more)
  • Form factor: rectangular 17.5 × 26.5 × 48.5 mm23 — physically incompatible with the cylindrical 18650 holders (18 × 65 mm)
  • Verdict: ❌ Inadequate on every axis (voltage, capacity, current delivery, form factor)

9V Li-ion rechargeable (EBL 5400mWh and similar) — rejected

  • Actual capacity: ~600 mAh (5400 mWh ÷ 9V)24 — the mWh label obscures a sub-alkaline mAh figure
  • Intended applications per manufacturer: “smoke alarms, microphones, toys, and medical devices”25 — all low-drain (mA-range) devices
  • Max discharge current not published; onboard protection circuit advertises over-current / short-circuit cutoff26 — will trip well below the 7A the compressor demands
  • Same 9V vs 7.4V voltage mismatch20 and same PP3 form factor incompatibility23 as the alkaline variant
  • Verdict: ❌ Designed for sub-1A low-drain devices, not for a 50W / 7A compressor

Decision

JESSPOW 3000mAh 30A (€14.40 for 2, Amazon.fr). After open search on Amazon.fr across multiple queries9, this is the only bare 18650 flat-top cell with an explicitly stated discharge rate. All other listings are either chargers, battery packs with connectors, or generic cells without discharge specs. Despite the 30A rating, the ATJ 8466 draws only 7A (2.3C on a 3Ah cell) — 23% of rated capacity — which is gentle on the cell and favorable for longevity.
