reading a SMART report

what
a USB disk, in this note, is a drive inside its USB enclosure, as its host sees it.
what
smartctl -H prints the drive’s own verdict, failing meaning failed or predicting its own failure within 24 hours1.
what
an attribute fails once its normalized VALUE falls to its THRESH, and a failed Pre-fail attribute means failure is imminent2.
what
attribute 5 counts the bad sectors found and reallocated3.
what
attribute 197 counts pending sectors, waiting to be reallocated after unrecoverable read errors4.
what
attribute 199 counts errors in data transfer over the interface cable5.
who
Backblaze recorded each day the SMART stats of the 67,814 drives in its data center, in 20166.
  • what: Backblaze holds as a reason to investigate a raw value above zero in attribute 5, 187, 188, 197 or 1987.
  • what: in that fleet, one of those five was above zero on 76.7% of failed drives and 4.2% of working ones8.

why so young a drive gave way

the drive is shingled

what
a shingled, SMR, drive writes each track over part of the one before, like roof shingles, to fit more tracks on a platter15.
  • what: a conventional, CMR, drive writes tracks that do not overlap15.
    • therefore: a CMR drive rewrites any block in place, touching nothing else.
  • what: on SMR, writing a track overwrites its neighbour, which must be rewritten too when it holds data15.
    • therefore: rewriting one block may cost an SMR drive rewriting the tracks after it.
  • what: continuous writing of large amounts of data is noticeably slower than on CMR drives15.
  • what: a device-managed SMR drive hides this in its firmware and shows to the host as a plain drive15.
what
Toshiba lists drive-managed SMR among the MQ04 series’ features, for the MQ04ABD200 among others16.
  • what: smartctl reports roddenberry’s old drive, an MQ04ABD200, as zoned, device managed, as the block below shows.
  • therefore: bimbap’s drive is SMR, by its maker’s word and by what the drive itself reports.

what

how people deal with disks that lie

what

therefore
the repair is a copy elsewhere, on another disk or another pi; checksums only tell which copy is bad.
