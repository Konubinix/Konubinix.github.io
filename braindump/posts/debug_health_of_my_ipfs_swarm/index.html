reading a SMART report

what
a USB disk, in this note, is a drive inside its USB enclosure, as its host sees it.
what
smartctl -H prints the drive’s own verdict, failing meaning failed or predicting its own failure within 24 hours1.
what
an attribute fails once its normalized VALUE falls to its THRESH, and a failed Pre-fail attribute means failure is imminent2.
what
attribute 5 counts the bad sectors found and reallocated3.
what
attribute 197 counts pending sectors, waiting to be reallocated after unrecoverable read errors4.
what
attribute 199 counts errors in data transfer over the interface cable5.
who
Backblaze recorded each day the SMART stats of the 67,814 drives in its data center, in 20166.
  • what: Backblaze holds as a reason to investigate a raw value above zero in attribute 5, 187, 188, 197 or 1987.
  • what: in that fleet, one of those five was above zero on 76.7% of failed drives and 4.2% of working ones8.

the swap experiments

how
change one part at a time: the hub with its cable first, for an official Raspberry Pi hub, then the USB disk cable.
  • why: the user bought the official Raspberry Pi hubs expecting them to be more reliable than this cheap one.
  • how: assuming a single fault, a reset after a change clears the part changed, and a quiet stretch well past the first 4 days points at it.
  • how: when the symptom shows, run the block below before any reboot, to tie it to a hub reset, and fill the row.
    • what: gooseberry’s journal is set to Storage=volatile, as the block just below shows, and so is kept only in memory11; a reboot wipes the reset that would explain the symptom.
    • what: a hub reset always brings the symptom into the user’s day, as the user states.

what to do about its drive

what
in Google’s fleet, drives were over 14 times likelier to fail within 60 days after their first reallocation, a count of the times a sector was reallocated13.
  • therefore: attribute 5 reads 301 on roddenberry, so at least one reallocation happened there.
what
there, drives were 16 times likelier to fail within 60 days after their first sector put on probation14.
  • therefore: probation resembles attribute 197’s pending sectors, an analogy the paper does not draw.
therefore
roddenberry’s drive sits in the reallocation group, and, if the analogy holds, in the probation group too.
how
