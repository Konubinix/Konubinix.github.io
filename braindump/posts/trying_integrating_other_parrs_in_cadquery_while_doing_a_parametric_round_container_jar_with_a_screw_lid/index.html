Trying Integrating Other Parrs in Cadquery While Doing a Parametric Round Container Jar With a Screw Lid

Fleeting

tl;dr
a round jar and the lid that screws onto it, every measure driven by the volume the jar has to hold.

the jar

what
the jar is a cylinder closed at the bottom, open at the top, and the lid screws onto that opening.
what
the cavity is what the jar is for, so its volume is the one measure chosen first and the rest follows from it.
what
the jar built here holds 5 cl.

the measures

what
inner_radius and inner_height are the cavity, and pi * inner_radius ** 2 * inner_height is the 5 cl it holds.
what
wall is the thickness of the shell, the bottom as thick as the side.
what
neck_od is the neck the lid grips, thread_od the crest of its thread and neck_height how far it stands above the jar, and so how deep the lid goes down.
what
pitch is how far one turn of the thread climbs.
what
neck_id is the mouth you pour through, which the fetched neck brings with it rather than takes: 21.74 is what it comes out at, and the body’s hole follows it.
what
rounding softens the rim the jar stands on.
what
lid_wall is the thickness of the lid’s shell.

inner_radius = 20
inner_height = 40
wall = 2
rounding = 2

neck_od = 30
thread_od = 34
neck_height = 12
pitch = 3
neck_id = 21.74

lid_wall = 2

the body

what
the body is the one part no library draws, being nothing but a cylinder hollowed to the cavity, closed top and bottom, and pierced through that top by the mouth.
what
the rim it stands on is rounded, so it sets down without a sharp edge.

outer_radius = inner_radius + wall
body_height = inner_height + 2 * wall

body = (
    cq.Workplane("XY")
    .cylinder(body_height, outer_radius, centered=(True, True, False))
    .edges("<Z")
    .fillet(rounding)
    .composecut(
        cq.Workplane("XY", origin=(0, 0, wall))
        .cylinder(inner_height, inner_radius, centered=(True, True, False))
    )
    .composecut(
        cq.Workplane("XY", origin=(0, 0, body_height - wall))
        .cylinder(wall, neck_id / 2, centered=(True, True, False))
    )
)

the neck

what
a screw thread that a lid actually catches is a solved problem, so the note takes one rather than drawing it.
  • why: this jar is a good occasion to try bringing the tooling of openscad into the awesomeness of cadquery.
what
PartCAD keeps a registry of parts, BOSL2’s bottle necks among them, so the note names the one it wants and partcad fetches it and hands it over as a solid.
what
the neck comes out standing on z = 0, which is the top of the body.

neck = partcad(
    "//pub/std/metric/bosl2/bottlecaps:generic_bottle_neck",
    neck_d=neck_od, thread_od=thread_od, height=neck_height, pitch=pitch)

jar = body.composeunion(neck.translate((0, 0, body_height)))

the lid

what
the cap is one of the few BOSL2 modules the registry does not publish, so the lid is fetched from BOSL2 itself, given the very measures the neck was given.
what
it is knurled, which is what you grip when you open the jar.

lid = scad("bottlecaps.scad").generic_bottle_cap(
    neck_od=neck_od, thread_od=thread_od, height=neck_height, pitch=pitch,
    wall=lid_wall, texture="knurled")

the printable models

what
the jar and the lid print apart, each one its own file.
