infinite loop when providing useless spec: {}

Consider the following configmap.

apiVersion: v1
kind: ConfigMap
metadata:
  name: something
spec: {}
data:
  someenv: somevalue

This will always be Out of sync. Argocd desiring to put spec: {} and k8s silently removing it because it does not make sense.
