tl;dr
a.blend

It worked ok, but I needed to add a bit of adhesive to make it fit. Otherwise, it tends to get out of the grip.

I guess that adding something on the top to make it fit better might be good.

In order not to print grips for every attempt, I added a dovetail mark on it.

My first attempt used 0.6 mm nozzle and no support on bambulab a1 mini. It was not great.

tthen, I tried 0.8, painted support only on the hanging part, no brim

It did not adhere at all.

Then, 0.8, auto tree support, it work-ish

adding a tripod mount screw

using https://www.printables.com/model/152911-camera-tripod-screw-14-20-unc

I just put it on top of the part that cut the dovetail in the grip.

using https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:918608

Adding a dove part and cutting it into the slider

Also increasing the height of the slider a little bit.

When printed like this, the dovetail part and the slider are quite hard to fit, but I managed. Once put together, they are very hard to remove.

I tried printing the slider and base à 105%, then the dovetail can be put and removed easily and it keeps the phone straight when playing at the vertical, but don’t shake too much !
