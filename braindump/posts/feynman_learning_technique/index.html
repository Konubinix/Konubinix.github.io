Feynman Learning Technique

Fleeting

  • External reference: https://sketchplanations.com/feynman-learning-technique

sketchplanations,

— https://sketchplanations.com/feynman-learning-technique

  • Spend time writing about a topic as if explaining it to someone.
  • Then practice explaining it, speaking and drawing as if you were a teacher at the front of the class. You may figure out some other aspect wasn’t as clear as you thought and need to look into something.
  • Refine any gaps you reveal until you can share the topic fluently and clearly.
  • Then, simplify your language by replacing complex words and ideas with simpler ones and considering analogies to help people relate.
