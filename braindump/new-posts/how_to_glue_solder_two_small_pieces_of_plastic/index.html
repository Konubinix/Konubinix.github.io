the ways to try for joining two small pieces of plastic — glue, weld or any other

rule
a way holds even when the pieces touch over only a few mm².
what
the two pieces are a snap hook and the molded part it broke off.
  • how: the hook flexes each time something clips onto it.
  • what: both of the molded part’s snap hooks broke off.
  • rule: both hooks are stuck back on.
  • rule: a hook is remade only if sticking it back on fails.
    • why: the user would rather not spend time perfecting the 3D model of the part if the hooks can be stuck back on.
what
the plastic of the pieces is unknown.
  • what: the molded part carries no recycling mark.
  • what: the user guesses ABS.

telling whether the plastic is ABS

what
for a plastic with no ID symbol, Polyvance gives the procedure below1, 2.
what
Polyvance says a rod that is not a proper match should pop off easily2.

cyanoacrylate, on ABS

what
in Henkel’s tests on one ABS grade, Loctite 414 (general purpose) and 401 (surface insensitive) cyanoacrylates bonded more strongly than the ABS itself3.
what
in the same tests, Loctite 380, a rubber-toughened cyanoacrylate, bonded ABS at 6.6 MPa4.
what
those tests bonded an overlap of 0.5 in², about 320 mm²5.
what
they loaded it in block shear, a method that minimizes peel and cleavage in the joint6.
therefore
these tests do not show how a cyanoacrylate holds over a few mm².
what
Henkel lists poor peel strength among the drawbacks of cyanoacrylates7.
  • what: Henkel says rubber-toughened cyanoacrylates offer high peel strength and impact resistance8.
what
uncured cyanoacrylate can stress-crack ABS9.
  • rule: excess cyanoacrylate is removed from the surface immediately9.

solvent welding, on ABS

what
solvent welding joins a plastic to itself or to another plastic that dissolves in the same solvent10.
what
Covestro says ABS parts are typically solvent-welded with MEK, acetone or a mix of the two11.
what
Covestro says a paste of MEK and the base resin can fill small gaps11.
what
Covestro says too much evaporation before clamping gives a poor weld12.
what
Covestro says that for critical applications needing more durability, glue is to be considered12.
what
Henkel says solvents are more likely to cause stress cracking than glues are13.

recasting the hook, on ABS

what
Polyvance’s PlastiFix kit replaces « a broken tab » by casting a new one in a mold taken from an undamaged piece14.
  • what: Polyvance speaks of « a broken tab », and its page does not say whether that covers a hook that flexes.
  • what: neither hook on the molded part is intact.
how
a FlexMold bar, softened in very hot water, is shaped over the pattern and, once cool, set in the repair area15.
how
the mold is filled with the PlastiFix powder/liquid mix, left to cure 30 minutes, then removed15.
what
Polyvance says the kit is ideal for ABS and other hard plastics, but does not work on PE, PP or TEO14.

heat welding, on ABS

what
for ABS, Polyvance lists welding with an ABS rod, by nitrogen/hot air or with an airless welder’s hot tip16.
how
the part is V-grooved halfway through, then the hot welder tip melts the rod into the groove and mixes it with the base material17.
what
its airless procedure pre-melts about two inches of plastic inside the groove17.
therefore
this procedure is not shown on a joint of a few mm².
what
Polyvance’s book comes from automotive plastic repair18.
what
in its hot-air welding, Polyvance says hot staples may also be used if needed to stabilize the crack19.
what
Polyvance says its staples can also be used to reinforce plastics20.

UHU Max Repair Extreme, which the user has

what
UHU says Max Repair Extreme repairs plastic among many materials21.
  • what: UHU says it is not suitable for PP, PE or PTFE22.
what
its data sheet gives a shear strength of 800 N/cm², that is 8 MPa23.
  • what: the data sheet does not say how, or over what area, that value was measured.
what
UHU describes it as extremely elastic24.
  • what: the same sheet’s specifications give its elasticity as limited25.
what
UHU lists it as resistant to shock and vibrations26.
how
glue goes on one side, the parts are pressed together, can be adjusted within 5 minutes, and reach full strength after about 24 hours27.
what
on the user’s hooks, the UHU did not hold at all.

epoxy, on ABS

what
the user has an epoxy resin used to smooth 3D prints, whose product is unknown.
  • what: the figures below are for Loctite epoxy adhesives.
what
in Henkel’s ABS tests, Loctite E-90FL, a flexible epoxy, bonded at 24.2 MPa, and E-20HP at 18.3 MPa28.
what
Henkel says their bonds did not break the ABS but performed exceptionally well29.
what
those tests bonded an overlap of 0.5 in², about 320 mm²30.
therefore
these tests do not show how an epoxy holds over a few mm².
what
Henkel says epoxies are typically rigid, and flexible ones need special formulation31.
what
Henkel lists long cure and fixture times among the drawbacks of epoxies32.
